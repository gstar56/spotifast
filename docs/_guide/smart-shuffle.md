Smart Shuffle plays a playlist, album or Liked Songs in a random order and
adds a song Spotify suggests for it after every three of your own. It plays
on this computer, so set up playback here first (see
[Getting started](/getting-started/)).

## Turn it on

Choose the **Shuffle** button in the player bar, or the one at the top of a
playlist or album page, until it shows a small sparkle. Each goes from off,
to shuffle, to Smart Shuffle, and back to off. The page's button sets the
mode for the next **Play** without starting anything. You can also switch
Smart Shuffle on from Spotify's app on your phone while this computer plays.

Suggested songs carry the same sparkle in the queue, so you can tell them
from the songs in your playlist. Songs you queue yourself still play first.
Suggestions never change the playlist itself.

## Good to know

- The first suggestions take a moment to arrive. Until they do, the queue
  shows plain shuffle.
- Spotify leaves out songs the playlist already has. If it has nothing new to
  suggest, the playlist plays as plain shuffle.
- Turning shuffle off ends Smart Shuffle too. Turning shuffle back on gives
  plain shuffle.
- Spotifast can't switch Smart Shuffle on for your other devices, because
  Spotify doesn't let other apps do that. When another device plays, the
  button only turns shuffle on and off. If Smart Shuffle was switched on
  there with Spotify's own app, the button still shows it.
- With [Spotify Soloist](/soloist/) as the player, Smart Shuffle works on
  playlists: Soloist adds the suggestions itself, and switching it restarts
  the playing song where it is. They don't carry the sparkle in the queue.
  On Liked Songs and albums the button offers plain shuffle there.
- Podcasts have no Smart Shuffle.

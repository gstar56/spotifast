A Jam lets several people listen to the same music and add songs to one
shared queue. It plays on this computer, so set up playback here first (see
[Getting started](/getting-started/)).

## Start a Jam

1. Play something, then choose the **Jam** button (two people) in the player
   bar.
2. Choose **Start a Jam**.
3. Choose **Copy invite link** and send it to your friends. They open it in
   any Spotify app to join.

As the host you can:

- Turn **Guests can play and skip** off, so guests can only add songs.
- Remove someone with the button next to their name.
- Choose **End Jam** to end it for everyone.

## Join a Jam

Paste an invite link into the Jam popup and choose **Join**, or open the link
with Spotifast. Both `open.spotify.com/socialsession/…` and `spotify.link/…`
links work. The Jam then plays on this computer, in sync with everyone else.

As a guest:

- **Next**, **Previous** and **Add to queue** go to the Jam, for everyone.
- **Pause** pauses only you. Press play to catch back up with the Jam.
- Choose **Leave Jam** to leave.

## Limits

Jam uses Spotify's private service rather than a public API, so Spotify can
change it without notice. It needs Spotify Premium, like all playback here.
See [How It Connects](/how-it-connects/#spotify-jam) for the details.

Spotifast 0.12.1 is the first release of this fork. It adds Spotify Jam and Smart Shuffle, fixes flashing in full screen lyrics, and from now on updates itself from this fork's releases.

**Download Spotifast:** [Windows](https://github.com/gstar56/spotifast/releases/download/v0.12.1/spotifast-v0.12.1-x86_64-pc-windows-msvc-setup.exe)

This fork builds for Windows on x86-64 only. It includes MilkDrop.

## New

- **Spotify Jam.** Start a Jam and share its `spotify.link` invite, which offers your friends the Spotify app. Or paste a friend's link to join their Jam and listen in sync on this computer. Skips and queued songs go to the Jam, and pausing pauses only you. Choose the Jam button in the player bar.
- **Smart Shuffle.** The shuffle buttons in the player bar and at the top of a playlist now go from off, to shuffle, to Smart Shuffle. Smart Shuffle adds a song Spotify suggests after every three of yours, marked with a sparkle in the queue. You can also switch it on from Spotify's app on your phone while this computer plays. It works while this computer is the player.
- **Updates from this fork.** Spotifast checks this fork's releases instead of upstream's, so an update never replaces it with a build without Jam.

## Fixed

- **Full screen lyrics no longer flash black** on screens narrower than the window's usual minimum, such as a portrait monitor or one at high display scaling. (crmne/spotifast#783)

It also carries everything on Spotifast's main branch since 0.12.0.

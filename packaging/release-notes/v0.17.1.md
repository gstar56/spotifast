Spotifast 0.17.1 brings this fork up to Spotifast 0.17 and adds Spotify DJ. Jam and Smart Shuffle carry over, and everything new in Spotifast 0.13 to 0.17 comes with them.

**Download Spotifast:** [Windows](https://github.com/gstar56/spotifast/releases/download/v0.17.1/spotifast-v0.17.1-x86_64-pc-windows-msvc-setup.exe)

This fork builds for Windows on x86-64 only. It includes MilkDrop.

## New

- **Spotify DJ.** DJ is back under Made for you on Home, and it plays here, with the DJ's spoken lines before and after songs, never over them. When a set runs out, the DJ picks the next one. Starting it from the top asks for a fresh set, and it never shuffles. It works while this computer is the player.
- **Everything in Spotifast 0.13 to 0.17.** Windows Media Player skins, Spotify Soloist, Cast, local files, MilkDrop in skins and behind your lyrics, and more. See [Spotifast's releases](https://github.com/crmne/spotifast/releases) for each one.
- **Spotify Jam and Smart Shuffle** work as in 0.12.1, now on Spotifast 0.17.

## Fixed

- **Full screen lyrics no longer flash black** on narrow or high-scaling screens. This now uses Spotifast's own fix in place of the fork's. (crmne/spotifast#892)

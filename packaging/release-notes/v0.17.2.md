Spotifast 0.17.2 adds crossfade: the end of a song blends into the next, the way Spotify's own apps do it.

**Download Spotifast:** [Windows](https://github.com/gstar56/spotifast/releases/download/v0.17.2/spotifast-v0.17.2-x86_64-pc-windows-msvc-setup.exe)

This fork builds for Windows on x86-64 only. It includes MilkDrop.

## New

- **Crossfade.** Choose how long songs overlap, up to 12 seconds, under **Settings → Playback on this computer**. A change applies from the next song on, without restarting playback. Only a song that ends by itself blends: a skip, a seek or a new playlist still cuts straight to the next song, and the DJ's spoken lines are never played over. It works while this computer is the player.

Spotifast 0.17.3 brings Spotify Jam, Spotify DJ and Smart Shuffle to Spotify Soloist, and stops full screen lyrics flashing black on some graphics cards.

**Download Spotifast:** [Windows](https://github.com/gstar56/spotifast/releases/download/v0.17.3/spotifast-v0.17.3-x86_64-pc-windows-msvc-setup.exe)

This fork builds for Windows on x86-64 only. It includes MilkDrop.

## New

- **Jam on Soloist.** With Soloist as the player, a Jam you start or join plays on Soloist, which handles it as Spotify's own apps do. Ending a Jam ends it for good.
- **DJ on Soloist.** Spotify's DJ starts on Soloist, its spoken lines included.
- **Smart Shuffle on Soloist.** The shuffle buttons reach Smart Shuffle on playlists while Soloist plays, and Soloist mixes in Spotify's suggestions. Switching it restarts the playing song where it is. On Liked Songs it stays plain shuffle, because Spotify doesn't take it there.

## Fixed

- **Full screen lyrics no longer flash black while the mouse moves** on some graphics cards, AMD's among them. The installer turns on Windows' **Disable fullscreen optimizations** for Spotifast, and removes it again on uninstall.
